QSOL-SUBSTRATE
==============

ROLE:
  Canonical public substrate.

VERB:
  KNOWS

RESPONSIBILITY:
  Publish inspectable public context and source-of-truth substrate records
  within its declared authority domain.

BOUNDARIES:
  SUBSTRATE_RECORD != UNIVERSAL_TRUTH
  PUBLIC != AUTOMATICALLY_CANONICAL
  DERIVED_PROJECTION != CANONICAL_SOURCE


QSOL-ORACLE
===========

ROLE:
  Evidence membrane.

VERB:
  WITNESSES

RESPONSIBILITY:
  Represent evidence state and preserve distinctions such as known,
  conflicted, unknown, observed, and unresolved.

BOUNDARIES:
  ORACLE_OUTPUT != UNIVERSAL_TRUTH
  EVIDENCE != CONSENSUS
  RETRIEVAL != ENDORSEMENT


QSOL-NEXUS
==========

ROLE:
  Reasoning and interpretation layer.

VERB:
  REASONS

RESPONSIBILITY:
  Reason over evidence, context, models, and deterministic tool receipts
  without redefining evidence or storage as truth.

BOUNDARIES:
  REASONING != EVIDENCE
  MODEL_CONSENSUS != TRUTH
  COUNCIL_VOTE != FACT

RESPONSIBILITY:

BOUNDARIES:
  COMPOSITION != SOURCE_AUTHORITY
  ADAPTER != CANONICAL_SOURCE

REVERSIBILITY
-------------

ARCHITECTURAL INVARIANT FAMILY:
  QSOL-REVERSIBILITY/1

R1:
  UNDO(APPLY(STATE, ACTION)) == STATE

R2:
  UNPACK(PACK(BYTES)) == BYTES

R3:
  DNA_DECODE(DNA_ENCODE(BYTES)) == BYTES

R4:
  speculative search MUST NOT mutate canonical state

R5:
  failed restoration MUST abort

STATUS NOTE:
  R2 and R3 correspond to reconstruction patterns in the recovery design.
  R1, R4, and R5 also define requirements for the planned deterministic
  search primitive below.

QSOL-ARK
========

ROLE:
  Recovery semantics and survivability.

VERB:
  SURVIVES

RESPONSIBILITY:
  Decide whether an available substrate satisfies a requested recovery
  capability under the declared Minimum Recoverable Substrate rules.

CONTROL may reconstruct bytes.
CONTROL does NOT decide that a reconstruction is sufficient.

MINIMUM RECOVERABLE SUBSTRATE:

  request capability
        |
        v
  inspect implemented recovery tiers
        |
        v
  choose lowest implemented sufficient tier
        |
        +-- none sufficient --> ARK_MRS_UNAVAILABLE
        |
        v
  recovery allowed

FINAL RULE
==========

THE SYSTEM DOES NOT ATTEMPT TO PRESERVE A MODEL'S SOUL.

IT PRESERVES ENOUGH EXPLICIT, INSPECTABLE, VERSIONED,
CONTENT-ADDRESSED CONTEXT FOR A FUTURE COMPATIBLE SYSTEM
TO RECONSTRUCT THE DECLARED WORKING RELATIONSHIP.

PROVE IT, MATE.
