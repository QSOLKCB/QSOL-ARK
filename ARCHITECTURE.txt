ROLE BOUNDARIES
---------------

  QSOL-SUBSTRATE
    role: canonical public substrate
    boundary: public substrate authority only within explicitly published payloads

  QSOL-ARK
    role: recovery semantics, Minimum Recoverable Substrate, archaeology and clean-room evaluation
    boundary: recovery sufficiency, not universal factual truth

  QSOL-INT
    role: composition and integration
    boundary: composition does not inherit source authority

  QSOL-ORACLE
    role: evidence membrane
    boundary: evidence state is not universal truth

  QSOL-NEXUS
    role: reasoning and interpretation
    boundary: reasoning is not evidence

  QSOL-CONTROL
    role: deterministic operational and reconstruction machinery
    boundary: operator machinery has no epistemic or recovery authority

  QSOL-CORPUS
    role: private provider-neutral interaction corpus
    boundary: archived messages and history are not facts or core identity

  QSOL-CONTEXT
    role: private curated working continuity
    boundary: context is not original model memory or a universal fact database

  LATTICE
    role: provider-neutral structural addressing and deterministic traversal
    boundary: geometry and position have no epistemic authority
